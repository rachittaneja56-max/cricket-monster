Selected direction: day / night

The all-navy direction made prediction cards, the slip and the quiz compete at the same visual level. The selected direction keeps the stadium and navigation dark, while warm chalk makes gameplay quieter and more readable. Leather red identifies the next action; green identifies the chosen answer and success. Gold is confined to the brand, team identity and small metadata.

Production contract questions

The approved demo remains eight Match questions and ten Daily questions / 180 seconds. The linked technical docs specify five Match questions with different range-based types, a five-question / 90-second Daily quiz, and a single match-start lock. These require a product decision before backend integration. No Groups, fake deadlines, live scores or ranking records were added.
